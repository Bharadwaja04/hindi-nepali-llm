Language Selection

Model H: Hindi
Hindi was selected as the higher-resource language because of the
availability of substantial public text resources across news,
literature, educational and general-domain sources.

Model L: Nepali
Nepali was selected as the lower-resource language because it is one
of the explicitly permitted lower-resource languages in the project.
The project will investigate how corpus scale, quality and tokenizer
characteristics affect a monolingual Nepali LM.
